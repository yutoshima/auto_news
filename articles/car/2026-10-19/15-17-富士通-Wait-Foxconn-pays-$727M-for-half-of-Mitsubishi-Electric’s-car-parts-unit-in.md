# 富士通? Wait: Foxconn pays $727M for half of Mitsubishi Electric’s car parts unit in EV push. We must translate accurately. Foxconn is the company; not 富士通. So: 富士通 is wrong. Translate:

「フォックスコン、三菱電機の自動車部品部門の半分を7.27億ドルで買収、電気自動車推進へ」 But 7.27億ドル equals $727M. "の半分を" is odd: it's half of Mitsubishi Electric’s car parts unit.
